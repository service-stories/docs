Service Stories turns completed service jobs into content, such as blog posts and social posts. With the API, you can read that content from your own code and publish it wherever you want, for example in WordPress.

## What you can do

- **List your companies** and their locations.
- **Read completed service tickets**: the jobs your stories are written from.
- **Read stories** with their full content, images and SEO fields, ready to publish.
- **Start story generation** for a ticket, the same as the **Generate** button in the app.

## Who it's for

- **Shops and service businesses** that want to publish their stories on their own website.
- **Agencies** that manage many companies and want to pull content for all of them with one API key.

## Base URL

Send every request to:

```text
https://api.servicestories.com/api/developer/v1
```

All requests and responses use JSON. IDs are strings. Dates are ISO 8601 strings, or `null`.
